Why year one is hard (we will say it plainly)

A licensing course teaches you how to pass a test. It does not teach you how to build a business, and most new agents are left alone to figure that out. The three things that end first years:

  • No repeat-business system. Every month starts at zero because nobody taught you how past clients and your sphere actually produce deals.
  • Inconsistent leads. A good week, then silence. Without a system, your income is weather.
  • Nobody to ask. It is 9 PM, you are mid-negotiation, and there is no one in your corner who has done this a few hundred times.

That honesty is our differentiator. We would rather show you exactly how the business works here, including the hard parts, than sell you a dream and watch you quit.

Fees: fixed, the same for everyone, and walked through face to face

Here is what we will tell you that most teams will not: our split schedule is fixed. It does not get negotiated agent by agent, there are no side deals, and the agent next to you works on the same schedule you do. We do not post the numbers publicly, for the same reason we do not negotiate them: the schedule is the schedule.

What we will do, before you sign anything, is sit down and walk through the exact numbers with you, alongside what the team share actually buys: your training and coaching, the opportunity and follow-up system, closing coordination on every file, and the marketing behind your listings. Bring your questions and a calculator. Most agents understand the math, next to the support, in about five minutes.

Business you generate from your own sphere and past clients always carries the most favorable split on the schedule. Team-provided opportunities carry a team share that reflects what they cost to produce. Fixed means fixed: nobody gets a better deal by asking louder.

Leverage Rewards: closings unlock support

As you close deals, you climb a simple ladder: Foundation, Growth, Scale, Elite, and Platform Pro. Every agent starts at Foundation, and moving up is your choice, at your pace. Each step unlocks leverage credits you can spend on the things that buy your time back: marketing, transaction coordination, and admin support. The point is not a trophy. It is that the more business you build, the more of the work around the business the team carries with you.

Proof, and what your first 12 months look like

Austin Jones joined this team in 2025 with zero closings to his name here. He ran the system below, out loud, with coaching, and finished that first year with 17 closings. Here is the path he ran:

Days 1-30: Foundation. Training, your database loaded, real conversations started. Both of your presentations, buyer and seller, learned and certified live. Gate: 150 people in your system, 60+ conversations.
Days 31-60: Momentum. Your own appointments, an open house every weekend, first client agreements signed, first offers written. Gate: 6+ appointments held, 2+ clients working with you.
Days 61-90: First contract. Negotiation and contract-to-close training while you convert your active clients. Gate: your first deal under contract, with 15+ people still in your pipeline.
Days 91-180: Build the pipeline. Your repeat-business system starts at your first closing. Gate: three more deals under contract, four total in six months, and a pipeline that covers next quarter.
Months 7-12: A business, not a hustle. Past clients who hear from you on purpose, referrals arriving because you asked at the right moments, and no month that starts at zero.

Who this is NOT for

  • Agents who want opportunities handed to them with no prospecting. The system feeds you, but you still make the calls. Everyone here does.
  • Agents shopping for a split and a desk. If the system, the coaching, and the accountability sound like overhead to you, a high-split brokerage with no support will make you happier.
  • Part-time dabblers. Year one on this path is a real commitment. We would rather tell you now than watch you drift.

If none of that scared you off, that is usually a good sign.

What should a new real estate agent look for in a real estate team?

Look for four things: a written training plan with milestones for your first 90 days, a real flow of opportunities plus a follow-up system to work them, a closing coordination partner so your time stays on people, and a leader who reviews your numbers and your deals with you every week.
