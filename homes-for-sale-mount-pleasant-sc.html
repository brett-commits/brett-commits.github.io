Homes for Sale in Mount Pleasant, SC

East of the Cooper, Mount Pleasant runs from the Old Village and harbor front to the big planned communities up Highway 41.

Mount Pleasant is really several markets stacked together: historic Old Village homes, marsh-front streets in Old Mount Pleasant, family neighborhoods like Park West and Dunes West, and newer sections in Carolina Park. Prices, fees, and flood exposure change street by street. This search shows every Mount Pleasant listing in the MLS as it stands today.
