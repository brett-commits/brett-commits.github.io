What is a buyer willing to pay for your home?

That is the only number that matters when you sell, and it is not the one an algorithm guesses. Enter your address for a valuation built on live local sales, then a real read from our team on what buyers would actually pay for your home, and what would move that number.

  • Built on what buyers actually paid. Closed sales around your home, not asking prices and not a national estimate.
  • A local team reads every one. We check your number against your street, your upgrades, and the buyers shopping your area right now.
  • Planning a move in the next year or so? Most people who check their value are. Knowing the real number early changes the repairs you make, the timing you pick, and where you land next.
