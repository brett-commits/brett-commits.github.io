Homes for Sale in Point Hope, Charleston, SC

Point Hope is Charleston's long-range master plan on the Cainhoy peninsula: thousands of acres, a growing list of builders, and schools that opened before most of the homes.

Multiple national builders are selling here now, alongside the first wave of resales, and the sections differ more than the marketing suggests: lot sizes, fees, and timelines all vary. Builder inventory that never reaches the MLS is a real factor in this community, which is why working with a team that tracks the builders directly matters. This search covers every Point Hope home listed in the MLS today.
