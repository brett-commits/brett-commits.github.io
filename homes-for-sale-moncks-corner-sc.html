Homes for Sale in Moncks Corner, SC

Moncks Corner is Berkeley County's small-town end: Lake Moultrie at the door, bigger lots, lower prices, and a commute that still lands under an hour.

Foxbank Plantation and the newer communities off Highway 52 drive most of the activity, with rural properties and lake-area homes rounding out the market. Buyers come here for space and value, and builders have followed them. This search shows every Moncks Corner listing in the MLS today.

Well, septic, and acreage questions show up often out here. We check the details on any property before you spend money on inspections.
