Join the Team / Wade Wawner

Wade Wawner

Real Estate Agent · The TREAT Team

Wade helps clients and families relocating to the Charleston Tri-County find the right fit, neighborhood by neighborhood. If you are moving here from somewhere else, he is the one who will tell you honestly how two areas compare for your commute, your schools, and your everyday life.

He is building his book the way this team asks every agent to: real conversations, a follow-up plan for every client, and a team behind him that includes a closing coordination partner on every file. He is also part of the team on a Summerville home currently under contract.

His standard is the team's standard: truth over comfort, and discovery before data, so the home you choose fits your life, not just your search filters.
