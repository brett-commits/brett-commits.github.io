Join the Team / Brett Kelley

Brett Kelley

Team Leader · The TREAT Team

Brett built his approach on a simple idea: this business can be done with integrity and still win. Since 2016 he has helped buyers and sellers across Charleston, Berkeley, and Dorchester counties, and today he leads The TREAT Team, a small team built around straight numbers and honest advice.

His standard is simple: know the numbers, tell the truth, and give every client a plan they understand. He is known for honest pricing conversations, including when the data calls for a price improvement or a better plan, and for walking clients through the same math when a home has been sitting on the market, so they can decide with confidence.

Brett also hosts the Living in Charleston SC channel on YouTube, where he breaks down neighborhoods, new construction, and the local market for people moving to the area.

He lives in Hanahan with his wife, Nina, their son, Bowen, and their dog, Millie. Off the clock he is usually on a golf course or outside somewhere in the Lowcountry.

Call or text the team at 843-738-2394.
