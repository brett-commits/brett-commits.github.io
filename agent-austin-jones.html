Meet the team / Austin Jones

Austin Jones

Buyer Agent · The TREAT Team

Austin joined The TREAT Team in 2025 and did in his first year what most agents never do: 17 closings, built one real conversation at a time. He works buyers across Charleston and Berkeley counties, from the first tour to the closing table.

His game is follow-through. Austin answers fast, keeps a tour honest at four to six homes that actually fit instead of a scattershot run through town, and stays on top of every deadline between contract and close. In November 2026 he helped a buyer close on a future home site in Mount Pleasant, the kind of purchase that takes patience and local knowledge to get right.

Buyers working with Austin get the full team behind him: pricing guidance from the team leader, and a dedicated closing coordination partner on every file.
