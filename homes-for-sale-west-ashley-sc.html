Homes for Sale in West Ashley, Charleston, SC

West Ashley is the closest you can live to downtown Charleston without paying downtown prices: ten to fifteen minutes across the Ashley River, in the city's oldest suburbs.

West Ashley is dozens of neighborhoods, not one. Avondale is the walkable heart, The Crescent and Byrnes Down are 1950s pockets close to the river, South Windermere sits by a West Ashley Greenway trailhead, and Carolina Bay, Grand Oaks, and Shadowmoss trade older character for newer space farther out. This search covers every listing in 29407 and 29414, live from the MLS.

The one thing we check first here is water. Parts of the Church Creek basin have flooded outside the mapped flood zones, so we pull the elevation, the insurance quote, and the drainage history on any West Ashley home before you write an offer. That homework is the difference between a good West Ashley deal and a bad one.
