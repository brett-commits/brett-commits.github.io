Condos for Sale in the Charleston Area

Every condo on the market across the Tri-County in one search: downtown Charleston, Mount Pleasant, West Ashley, Daniel Island, and the beach towns.

A condo is a building decision as much as a home decision. The monthly HOA dues, what they cover, the rental rules, and the building's reserve health decide what the home really costs and who can buy it next. Two condos at the same price in different buildings can be hundreds of dollars a month apart.

On any condo from this list, we pull the HOA dues, what they include, and the rental rules before you tour, so you compare buildings with the full monthly picture in front of you.
